FAQ

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything (exam selections, progress, notes, and scores) is stored only in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to any server. PDFs are kept on your device too.

Where is my data on another device?

Data stays on the device where you entered it. Use Export to download a JSON backup and Import it on a new device to restore everything.

Reset

Remove all data and start fresh. This cannot be undone.

Export / Import

About

Exam Countdown Planner
Browser-only planner for Singapore national exams (GCE O/NA/NT/A-Level). All data stays on your device.

Grade boundaries shown throughout are estimates. Cross-check with your teachers. Official SEAB dates: seab.gov.sg
